Water Heater Replacement in York

A leaking tank can't be patched. Once the steel inside has corroded through, the only fix is a new heater, and we can often install a standard tank the same day or the next. You'll get a clear quote first, plus a code check of the venting, the gas or electrical supply, and the relief valve piping.

  • A tank more than 10 years old
  • Rust and corrosion on the tank or fittings
  • Needing more hot water than the old tank delivers
  • Water leaking from the bottom of the tank

Recognize These Water Heater Warning Signs?

Water heater trouble can appear without warning. If you're facing any of these problems, contact our team right away for expert help.

Water Heater Not Heating

A tank that stops heating is the call we run most. Our trucks carry thermocouples, elements, and thermostats to restore hot water the same day.

Fast repair help

Water Around the Heater

A dripping temperature and pressure relief valve is a warning sign, not a nuisance. It usually means excess pressure or heat, often from a missing expansion tank.

Leak repair

Strange Sounds and Odd Smells

A rotten-egg smell in the hot water usually comes from bacteria reacting with the anode rod. Swapping to a different anode and flushing the tank typically fixes it.

Water heater maintenance

Aging Tank, Mounting Repairs

Rust at the fittings, a spent anode rod, and water pooling under the tank are signs the smart money is on replacement.

Replacement options

Tankless Unit Acting Up

Hot-cold-hot water, a unit that shuts down mid-shower, or a flashing code usually points to mineral scale. A proper descaling often solves it.

Tankless repair
