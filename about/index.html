Who We Are

Built on Straight Answers

York Water Heater focuses on water heaters for houses, apartments, and commercial buildings across the area. We repair gas and electric tanks, install and service tankless units, set up heat pump water heaters, and handle the urgent calls, like a leaking tank or a house with no hot water.

We believe in doing the job right the first time, treating every customer's home with respect, and being upfront about cost. When a repair makes sense, we say so, even if a new heater would be a bigger ticket. New installs get the details that matter: a properly piped relief valve, correct venting, an expansion tank where required, and a full leak and temperature check.

We work in this community every day, so every job is a chance to earn a referral. It's a simple way to run a business, and it works: do good work, be honest, and people call back.
